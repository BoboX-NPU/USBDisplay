# MacBook and iPhone USB-C Icon Concept

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-07. The user's supplied MacBook and iPhone pictograms informed the silhouettes. The final image was generated from the text prompt below after two edits produced an unwanted transparent patch beneath the laptop.

Asset: [PNG concept](usb-display-icon-macbook-iphone-v3.png). This remains a design concept rather than an installed application icon or production icon set.

## Final Generation Prompt

```text
Create a NEW flat vector-style application icon from scratch, square composition. A bright solid blue rounded-square tile on transparent exterior.
Inside the tile: an open MacBook on the left and a vertical iPhone on the right. MacBook silhouette is a thick dark-navy rounded rectangular screen frame with a small top-center notch, blank pale-blue screen, and one slim dark-navy horizontal base extending a little beyond the screen at both ends. No desktop monitor stand. The phone has a matching dark-navy rounded frame, blank pale-blue screen, small navy Dynamic Island pill and navy home indicator.
A single thick white USB-C cable connects the right end of the MacBook base to the bottom-center of the iPhone. Its rounded white connector at the MacBook faces LEFT into the side of the base, then the cable curves down in a neat U below the two devices and returns up into the iPhone. A compact white USB-C connector at each end.
Very simple clean pictograms inspired by Apple hardware symbols. Flat solid colors ONLY. Every region uses one uniform color. No gradients, NO SHADOWS ANYWHERE, no texture, no blur, no lighting, no 3D, no text, no logos. The blue tile is completely solid and opaque throughout its full interior with no hole or transparency beneath the laptop or cable. Only outside the tile is transparent. Center the device-and-cable group inside the tile with generous even margins. Crisp clean silhouette edges.
```
