# MacBook Side-Port USB-C Icon Concept

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-07 in two passes. The fresh composition exposes the MacBook's right chassis wall to make its USB-C port position visible. The refinement simplifies the hardware to flat geometric shapes. The opaque blue square avoids the previous concept's transparent-edge artifacts.

Asset: [PNG concept](usb-display-icon-macbook-iphone-v4.png). This is an opaque design source, not an installed application icon or production icon set. The earlier rounded transparent concepts remain separate files.

## Initial Generation Prompt

```text
Create a completely NEW, crisp flat-vector application icon for a MacBook-to-iPhone USB-C screen mirroring app. Square image, full-bleed solid blue background (#168AF3) covering every pixel edge-to-edge. This is an opaque app-icon master: no transparency, no cutout, no outer margin or rounded outer tile, no checkerboard.

Two simple clean device pictograms occupy the middle of the square. An open MacBook on the left, slightly angled to show the RIGHT SIDE of its actual lower keyboard chassis. Its screen has a rounded dark-navy bezel, a small centered top notch, and a blank uniform pale-blue screen. Its thin lower base is a simple shallow quadrilateral with just a tiny trackpad outline, enough to clearly show a real RIGHT SIDE edge. Keep all surfaces flat solid color, without gradients, illumination, or rendering effects. No monitor stand or iMac chin.

An upright front-facing iPhone stands on the right, dark-navy rounded frame, uniform pale-blue screen, small Dynamic Island pill, and a home indicator.

CORRECT USB-C CABLE PLACEMENT: show a small USB-C port on the MacBook's RIGHT SIDE WALL of the lower keyboard chassis, near the hinge. One white USB-C plug is visibly seated sideways in that SIDE port. The plug MUST NOT emerge from the display bezel, the bottom of the screen, the front lip, the middle of the front edge, or the far front corner. The cable runs out from the side port to the right, curves down smoothly into a compact U below the devices, and runs up into a white USB-C plug at the EXACT BOTTOM CENTER of the iPhone. One cable and two plugs only. Visually traceable uninterrupted connection, no overlap across screens.

Simple bold geometric outlines with rounded corners. Four-color palette: uniform blue background, navy frames, pale-blue screens, white cable. Generous balanced spacing. Razor-clean smooth antialiasing, no mosaic, pixel noise, fringe, texture, grain, blur, shadow, glow, gradient, photographic realism, lettering, Apple logo, watermark, or added decoration.
```

## Final Refinement Prompt

```text
Refine this MacBook-and-iPhone icon into the very simple flat pictogram style requested. Preserve the corrected connection exactly: the cable plugs horizontally into the visible right SIDE WALL of the MacBook lower base near the hinge, then curves into the iPhone's bottom center. Keep both device positions and the uninterrupted white cable.

SIMPLIFY THE MACBOOK: remove ALL individual keyboard keys, metallic rendering, silver texture, highlights, and trackpad detail. Replace its keyboard deck with ONE flat uniform pale-blue shape, and its thin visible side/front wall with ONE flat dark-navy shape matching the screen frame. The only deck detail may be a single small navy rounded-rectangle trackpad outline. The MacBook has a thick navy screen border and small top notch. Maintain just enough angled base geometry to make the side USB-C port location unambiguous. Keep the phone equally simple with navy frame, blank pale-blue screen, top pill and home indicator. Remove phone side-button details.

Use only FOUR solid uniform colors across the entire image: #168AF3 background, #102C52 frames/base edge, #CBE8FA screens/deck, #FFFFFF cable/plugs. Absolutely no gradient, grain, texture, shadows, illumination, blur, simulated materials, or mosaic. Clean smooth antialiased geometry. Background remains an opaque full-bleed blue square, with no transparency and no rounded outer mask. No words, logos, or other objects.
```
