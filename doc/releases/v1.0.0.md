# USB Display 1.0.0

The first public release of USB Display: a native macOS app for viewing a USB-connected iPhone or iPad, with no companion app or account.

## Downloads

- [DMG — Apple Silicon and Intel](https://github.com/BoboX-NPU/USBDisplay/releases/download/v1.0.0/USBDisplay-1.0.0-macOS-universal.dmg)
- [ZIP — Apple Silicon and Intel](https://github.com/BoboX-NPU/USBDisplay/releases/download/v1.0.0/USBDisplay-1.0.0-macOS-universal.zip)
- [SHA-256 checksums](https://github.com/BoboX-NPU/USBDisplay/releases/download/v1.0.0/SHA256SUMS.txt)
- [Source at v1.0.0](https://github.com/BoboX-NPU/USBDisplay/tree/v1.0.0)

The app targets macOS 14+, includes `arm64` and `x86_64`, and reports version 1.0.0 (build 4). Install the app in Applications and connect an unlocked device with a USB data cable. [Installation and troubleshooting](https://github.com/BoboX-NPU/USBDisplay/blob/v1.0.0/doc/installation.md).

## Included

- Wired video preview, proportional resizing, independent device windows, and native full screen.
- Resizable sidebar, compact hover controls, connection/recovery states, and automatic model/corner profiles.
- Native layered white application icon.
- MIT-licensed source, public issue/PR templates, contributor documentation, and a documented maintenance/release workflow.

## Signing and known limitations

**These downloads are ad-hoc signed and are not notarized by Apple.** They do not have a Developer ID Application signature, and Gatekeeper can block the initial launch. Review the installation instructions and [Apple's first-launch guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445); do not disable system-wide protections.

Startup delays and intermittent black previews remain under investigation. Protected content may also appear black. Runtime coverage on older macOS versions, Intel hardware, additional mobile devices, and prolonged multi-window sessions remains incomplete. Continuous live-video interaction and performance are not certified by the automated tests.

There is no recording, audio capture, remote transmission, mobile-device control, or built-in updater. The app's catalog covers 164 model identifiers; that does not mean 164 devices have been physically tested.

## Validation

The release is checked with the project's Debug tests and catalog checks, plus a universal Release build, archive integrity, signature integrity, architecture/version/resource checks, and package checksums. Detailed results and the limits of physical acceptance are recorded in [current project state](https://github.com/BoboX-NPU/USBDisplay/blob/v1.0.0/doc/current.md) and the [development log](https://github.com/BoboX-NPU/USBDisplay/blob/v1.0.0/doc/devlog.md).
